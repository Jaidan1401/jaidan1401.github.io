Robocide

2024

Unreal Engine Blueprint 3D Finished

Robocide was a duo project with me working on the engine side of the game and my friend Harley on the modelling end.

The game is an ULTRAKILL-inspired boomer shooter in which you play as a rogue robot trying to take down the forces that created you. The first section supplies the player with a weapon and introduces them to basic enemy encounters, before ramping up and introducing new enemy variants to look out for. The second section is a procedurally generated maze on rooftops that requires the player to defeat a specific amount of enemies in order to open the way to the boss tower. The game ends once the boss has been defeated.

Working on this project was quite a learning experience as it pushed me to learn how to make use of Unreal's stencil masks for applying post-processing effects to specific objects, along with creating weak points on enemies and how to utilize procedural generation.

Due to time restrictions, portions of the play area aren't as polished as the rest of the environment.
