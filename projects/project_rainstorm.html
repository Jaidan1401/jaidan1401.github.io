Project Rainstorm

2025

Unreal Engine Blueprint 3D Finished

Project Rainstorm was my final creation for College, following the brief of choosing a series to make a game about. I ended up choosing the Risk of Rain franchise as I was playing it a lot during this time, and I was specifically inspired by Risk of Rain 2 since I felt more comfortable creating 3D games.

My initial concept for this project was surviving in a hostile environment (referred to as the void in the Risk of Rain franchise) and going out to scavenge for items to assist in warding off enemies and fortifying the safe zone. I originally planned for two playable characters, but due to time constraints and my priorities being on the core gameplay I only went through with making one.

In order to mimic the playstyle of Risk of Rain 2, I started by creating a third-person character controller and inputs for primary/secondary/utility/special skills. The character I ended up creating is a soldier known as the Operative. His primary means of damage comes from his pistol, although he also comes with a melee option and an evasive skill. The actual gameplay turned out differently to my original plan as I couldn't find an enjoyable way to execute my concept, though the core premise is still there. The player starts in a safe zone and from the void come enemies that must be defeated to level up and potentially drop rare items that have unique properties - these items can also be found around the general area in the form of crates, but require venturing out into the void which can slowly kill the player if left unchecked.

If I were to re-do this now, I would definitely try sticking more to my original concept. The void doesn't serve as much of a threat as I intended it to be, since items to aid in survivability could easily be collected en masse. I would remedy this by toning down the item rates or rebalancing them completely, and I would increase the size of the playable area to encourage exploration.

All assets except for the text font were made by me using Blender, Substance Designer, jsfxr and Unreal's material editor.
