Avaritia

2024

Unreal Engine Blueprint 2.5D Finished

Avaritia was the result of a College group project about a problem in the world, and I was responsible for most of the programming and engine work.

The game is a 2.5D platformer with puzzles spread throughout inspired by Little Nightmares. You play as 22b, a girl from the slums of the city of Avaritia - the goal being to progress through the city until you can find an escape. But it isn't so easy, along the way you'll be faced with resistance in the form of a robotic police force commanded by the rich to keep order.

I created the character controller and put all the levels together. I attempted to use level streaming but my implementation of it was flawed which is why the screen temporarily turns black in specific areas, though I have since learned how to correctly make use of level streaming.

Most assets were created by others in the group, though I made a few minor props as the project required everyone make at least 1 puzzle, model and piece of concept art. Some sounds were sourced from FreeSound.
