"Duality" Game Jam Project

2024

Unreal Engine Blueprint 2.5D Finished

My College lecturers held an internal 5-day-long game jam with the prompt "Duality". My take was to have a character that can switch between two states to help with different aspects such as movement or damage. The player's "dark form" is capable of outputting more damage but moves slower as a result, and their "light form" does less damage but is able to maneuver faster and heals upon hitting an enemy.

I decided on an isometric 2.5D view after tinkering with the Unreal Engine camera settings until I found a result I thought was interesting. Most of the gameplay revolves around defeating enemies or pressing buttons to open up subsequent rooms until the player reaches the end of the level. I managed to create 3 basic levels of increasing difficulty within the time frame.

Reflecting back on this project, I feel I could've made the "Duality" theme more present, as the light form is only really used to run around faster and the dark form takes center stage. If I did this again I would maybe have specific areas only accessible by one of the forms, or enemies that can only be damaged by their corresponding form.
