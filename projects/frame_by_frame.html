Frame-by-Frame Fighter

2026

Unity C# 2D Finished

This is one of two prototypes I created for a module dedicated to rapidly concepting and prototyping ideas. The specific brief for this was to make a prototype only using one button and one set of directional inputs, and my take on this was to make this project entirely mouse-based.

The concept itself was a 2D fighting game where you must plan out your actions before performing them in real time. My original idea was to plan movements literally frame-by-frame, requiring the player to lay out up to 24 movements and then playing each of them back once everything was confirmed. The player would be punished for or otherwise restricted from trying to attempt movements that wouldn't be physically possible, like moving too far from their starting point or changing their velocity too drastically - similar to the abilities of the characters Naobito Zenin and Naoya Zenin from the anime Jujutsu Kaisen.

I ended up scrapping the more hands-on, frame-by-frame approach as it proved too tedious while testing. Instead, I opted for cyclable pre-defined actions that can be previewed and performed in a set amount of time. To fit the brief, actions are cycled by pressing left click and performed by holding left click while the direction the character travels in is determined by the position of the mouse on the screen.

For prototyping purposes there is only one playable character and the enemy is a simple AI capable of choosing actions based on limited parameters, such as distance and whether they are above or below the player.
