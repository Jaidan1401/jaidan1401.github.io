DELTARUNE Combat Recreation

2026

Unity C# 2D Finished

My first proper introduction to Unity was through this project. Seeing as I wanted to get used to Unity and C# I thought it best to recreate something I have a massive appreciation for, that being DELTARUNE.

I originally was going to create a party/battle system inspired by the game, but after receiving permission to use the game's assets I decided to make it a more direct recreation. I started this project by modifying a framework provided to me by my University to create a party system where two non-playable teammates would follow behind the player character and their health would display at the bottom. I then went straight into making a battle system that forces each party member to go to a specific point on the screen and activates the accompanying interface.

The battle system itself was made in around 4 weeks. My first priority was being able to select specific actions such as attacking, acting/casting spells, and defending. I didn't end up fully recreating the player attacking system or items as I wanted to prioritize the enemy's attack patterns in order to create a more complete feeling experience, what use is a battle system if there is no opposition?

The enemy I recreated specifically is the Roaring Knight, the overarching antagonist of the game that first appears near the end of the third Chapter. I chose this enemy because their attacks are all unique and would push me to learn multiple different aspects of the Unity engine, such as the first attack teaching me how to utilize sprite masks - something I then used for the rest of the encounter.

I feel like I could have polished the experience more if I allocated more time to working on systems that were ultimately neglected, like the lack of character dialogue and items. Despite these shortcomings, I am very proud of how this ended up turning out, and I am honored to have been permitted to use assets from a game I love.
